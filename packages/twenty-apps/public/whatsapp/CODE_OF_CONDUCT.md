# Code of Conduct

We expect project participation to remain professional, respectful and focused on improving the software.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful in technical disagreement.
- Give actionable, evidence-based feedback.
- Respect privacy and never publish customer credentials or personal data.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, threats or abusive behavior.

## Enforcement

Serious or repeated violations may result in comments, issues, pull requests or participation being restricted by the maintainers.

For private conduct concerns, contact hello@nextpagetechnologies.com.
